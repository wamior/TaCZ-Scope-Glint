# Icon source

The icon is a crop of an actual Minecraft 1.21.1 screenshot captured with F2 on 20 November 2026.
The game was running NeoForge 21.1.248, TaCZ 1.2.0-hotfix and TaCZ Scope Glint 1.1.0.
The screenshot shows an M700 with an 8x optical attachment and the addon glint in a local test world.

Original capture: `assets/scope-glint-gameplay.png`.
Crop: left 100, top 50, right 900, bottom 850, without adding or painting any pixels.
The 512px and 256px variants use ordinary Lanczos resampling. No image generator was used for these files.

The initial image_gen concept was discarded and is not included in the project or its release jars.

Minecraft artwork belongs to Mojang/Microsoft. TaCZ gun/scope artwork belongs to its respective authors.
This is an in-game screenshot of the addon, not an official Minecraft or TaCZ logo.
